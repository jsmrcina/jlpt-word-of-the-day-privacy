Privacy Policy: JLPT Word of the Day

Last updated: November 9, 2026

JLPT Word of the Day (“the app”) is a Japanese vocabulary app for Android.

Data the app collects

None. The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or usage data. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting, and it does not request internet access.

Data stored on your device

To work, the app keeps the following only on your device:

  • which words you have been shown, and your favorites, “known” words and custom word lists;
  • your settings (word lists, display options, notification time).

This data never leaves your device through the app. If Android’s device backup is turned on, Android may include it in your own Google backup, as with any app; the bundled dictionary is excluded. You can delete everything with Settings → Your data → Reset all data, or by uninstalling the app.

Files you export (Anki CSV) are saved where you choose and are then under your control.

Permissions

  • Notifications (optional): only for the daily word notification, if you turn it on.
  • Run at startup / keep awake briefly: used by Android’s WorkManager library to update the home-screen widget and to restore the daily alarms after a restart.

Links

Links in the app (jisho.org, Tatoeba, licence pages) open in your browser. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page with a new date. For questions or requests about privacy, use the developer contact email shown on the app’s Google Play page.
